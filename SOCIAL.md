## 4. Content Pillars

Rotate across these five. Each post must map to at least one.

| Pillar | Purpose | Example formats |
|---|---|---|
| **Capability** | Educate on the six disciplines | Carousel breaking down one discipline, 60s explainer |
| **Perspective** | Demonstrate authority on transformation | Leadership commentary, contrarian observations on enterprise tech |
| **Proof** | Show delivery and engineering culture | Build snapshots, architecture walkthroughs, screenshots, event photos |
| **People** | Establish the team as practitioners, not marketers | Team features, hiring posts, engineering practice posts |
| **Position** | Reinforce the positioning itself | Proposition graphics, industry focus, international standards |
